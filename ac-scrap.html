How to Describe AC Scrap for an Assessment

A clear equipment inventory helps the team understand what is included in your enquiry, especially when indoor and outdoor units or multiple systems are involved.

Identify each system

Note whether the equipment is a split or window AC, cassette, ductable system, VRV/VRF setup or chiller. If you know the make, model or capacity, include it.

Count the components

For split and multi-unit systems, say how many indoor and outdoor units are present and whether the units are together or at different locations.

Describe the condition

Mention if the equipment is working, damaged, dismantled or incomplete. Photos can help clarify what is included; final assessment and price are discussed with the team.

For chillers or plant rooms

Share the approximate number of machines, the site location and whether other HVAC equipment is part of the clearance. Large equipment enquiries are reviewed individually.
